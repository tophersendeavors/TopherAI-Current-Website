Section 5

The after-hours advantage

Call businesses after they close — on purpose.

Say it exactly like this"I'm actually calling after hours intentionally. I reached your voicemail, and that's exactly why I wanted to introduce myself."

Why this works

If the call reaches voicemail, you are experiencing the exact problem Topher AI solves. Instead of telling the company that missed calls are a problem, they just demonstrated it themselves.

Never insult their system

Never insult their voicemail, receptionist, or existing setup. Do not say:

  • "Your phone system is terrible."
  • "You just lost a customer."
  • "Nobody answers your phone."

What never to say

Never say:

  • "The AI never makes mistakes."
  • "It can handle everything."
  • "You won't need employees."
